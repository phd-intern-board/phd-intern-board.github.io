About this board

Last updated 2026-11-02

PhD Intern Board is a daily list of research internships, fellowships, student researcher roles, AI residencies and predoctoral positions at AI labs, big tech and startups. It exists because general internship lists are dominated by software engineering roles, and because research postings often close soon after they appear, which makes checking many careers pages by hand impractical.

Where the listings come from

Every day the board reads the public job boards of more than 100 hand-picked labs and companies, through the public APIs of their applicant tracking systems. Each of those boards is read in full, so an empty result for a lab really does mean nothing relevant is open there that day. Community-maintained internship data fills in big tech companies that have no public API, and also surfaces companies nobody thought to add.

Some labs only announce on X or on their own site. Those roles are added by hand and labelled by where they came from. The strip of announcements at the top of the board shows such posts exactly as they were written.

What gets included

  • In: research-focused roles for students and early-career researchers: research internships, student researcher positions, fellowships, residencies and predoctoral roles.
  • Out: general software engineering internships and senior full-time roles.

The filter works on titles and descriptions, so it makes mistakes in both directions. Treat the employer's own page as the final word on eligibility, dates and requirements.

How to read a listing

Listings are grouped by kind of organisation (frontier lab, big tech, startup, infra & chips, research org), because that is usually how people decide where to apply. New roles are flagged, and every listing links straight to the employer's own page. This site does not take applications and is not affiliated with any employer listed.

Using the data

The listings are also available as an RSS feed and as free static JSON, documented in the source repository.

Corrections

Missing lab, wrong listing or a role that should be removed: open an issue on GitHub. See also the privacy notice.
